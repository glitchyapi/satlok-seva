Install on Android

  1. Tap Download APK above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will say the source is unknown — that is normal for an app outside the Play Store.
  3. Tap Settings → Allow from this source, then Install.
  4. Open the app. Future updates arrive inside the app itself.
Requires Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) or newer. The APK is under 1 MB and works fully offline after install.

Privacy

  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to verify.
  • No server storage. Your seva is written only to your own device.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers. The only network request the app makes is checking the update manifest.
  • Encrypted backups. Export a single .ssvb file locked with a PIN you choose; restore it on any phone.
  • Signed releases. Every update manifest is RSA-2048 signed and verified against a key pinned inside the app.
